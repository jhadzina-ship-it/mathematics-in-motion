How a lesson works

Every lesson is one idea, taught the same way.

1

Watch

A short animation, about two minutes, that builds the picture step by step so the result feels inevitable rather than memorised.

2

Practise

Four to five questions on the page. Type a number, press check, and get feedback straight away. A hint appears after a miss.

3

Master

Worked solutions open when you need them. Get them all right and the lesson is done. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for.
