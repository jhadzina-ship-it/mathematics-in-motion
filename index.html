How a lesson works

Step 1

Watch

A short animation, about two minutes, that builds the picture step by step. The first practice question uses the numbers from the video, so watch to the end.

Step 2

Read

The result in one sentence, with the explanation written out and the syllabus objective it meets, ready to copy into your notes.

Step 3

Practise

Calculation questions, like the exam. Type your answer, and type or photograph your working to have it marked with feedback. A hint appears after a miss.

Step 4

Pass 80%

Score 80% or more and the lesson is complete, which opens the next lesson in that topic. Your progress is saved in this browser, no sign-up needed.

Finished a topic? Test yourself.

Take a short test with new numbers on everything in the topic. The report tells you which lessons to go back to before the exam.
