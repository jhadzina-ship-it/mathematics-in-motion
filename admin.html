    New lesson

    Everything the assistant drafted, field by field. Topic and chapter are set from the syllabus topic when you save.

    Basics

    Numbered by syllabus topic, e.g. 10.2 is the second Trigonometry lesson.

    Paste the whole link; the id is extracted.

    Advanced: web address (slug)

    Lowercase letters, numbers and dashes. Filled from the title if left empty. Saving with an existing slug updates that lesson.

    Syllabus

    Decides the chapter and the lesson number.

    Content

    Paste an image address, or choose a file to upload it.

    Questions

    Students type a number. Give the exact answer and how close counts (tolerance). Hints show after a miss; the worked solution opens after two. The first question should be the video check: its numbers come straight from the video.

    Not attached

    YouTube Studio → Content → open the video → Subtitles → on the English (automatic) row open the ⋮ menu → Download (.srt or .sbv), then attach the file. Or on the video page press …more → Show transcript, copy it, and paste it below. Your own script works just as well. The assistant uses it to write the explanation and questions from what you actually say in the video.

    Nothing goes live until you press Post as Live or say "post it". Enter sends, Shift+Enter makes a new line.
