About

Jephias Hadzina

Jephias Hadzina has taught high school mathematics for 10 years. He holds a Diploma in Education from Mutare Teachers' College and a BSc Honours in Mathematics from Bindura University of Science Education (BUSE), and is now studying for an MA in Mathematics at Yeshiva University, New York.

What Mathematics in Motion is

Free animated video lessons for the Zimbabwe O-Level, Forms 3 and 4, following ZIMSEC Mathematics Syllabus B. Each lesson shows a result happening on screen, explains it in plain words, and then gives you practice questions whose working is marked with feedback. Finish a lesson's practice and the next lesson in the topic opens. No sign-up, no fee.

The project

Content

Jephias writes, animates and voices every video, and writes the questions and worked solutions.

Site

Built by Kudzai Madembo as a free static site, so it costs nothing to run and Jephias can post new lessons himself.
