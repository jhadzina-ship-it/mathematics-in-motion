About

Jephias Hadzina

Mathematics teacher and animator. Mathematics in Motion is his project to teach secondary-school mathematics through visualisations, animations and real-world situations.

The idea

Most students meet a theorem as a sentence in a textbook and a diagram that never moves. Jephias animates the diagram. When a point slides around a circle and an angle stays the same, the theorem stops being something to memorise and becomes something you have seen happen. The target student is about twelve, strong at mathematics, and bored by it. The aim is to make them excited.

Background

Jephias taught the Zimbabwe O-Level syllabus (Forms 3 and 4), where the first videos come from. He is now a graduate student at the Katz School of Science and Health, Yeshiva University, New York.
