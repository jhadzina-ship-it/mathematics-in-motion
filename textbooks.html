Where each lesson fits your textbook

The videos were written for the Zimbabwe O-Level syllabus (Forms 3 and 4). US geometry books teach the same results, sometimes under different names and always in a different order. This page maps one to the other.

Three widely adopted US geometry textbooks

Each is the Geometry volume of an Algebra 1 / Geometry / Algebra 2 series. Older editions are cheap second-hand and the chapters in question change little between editions.

Savvas

enVision Geometry

Part of enVision A|G|A. Problem-based, visual lessons. EdReports rated the 2018 edition as meeting expectations for alignment to the Common Core standards.

Big Ideas Learning

Big Ideas Math: Geometry

Larson and Boswell. Distributed by National Geographic Learning / Cengage. A widely used author team; check the EdReports rating for the edition you hold.

HMH

HMH Into Geometry

Part of Into AGA (2020). EdReports: meets expectations for alignment, with built-in formative checks and intervention suggestions.

Same theorem, two names

Commonwealth syllabuses (Zimbabwe, the UK, Singapore) say alternate segment theorem; US texts say the angle formed by a tangent and a chord is half the intercepted arc. Angles of elevation are named the same on both sides, but US books meet them in a right-triangle trigonometry chapter rather than a separate topic.
